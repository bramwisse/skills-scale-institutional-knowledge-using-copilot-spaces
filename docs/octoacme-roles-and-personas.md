# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Core Delivery Roles

### Developers

#### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

#### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

#### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

#### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

### Product Managers

#### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

#### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

#### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

#### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

### Project Managers

#### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

#### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

#### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

#### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

## Cross-Functional Supporting Roles

### UX Designer

#### Role Summary
UX Designers ensure that features are intuitive, accessible, and deliver a superior user experience. They collaborate with Product Managers and Developers to translate user needs into practical design solutions.

#### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create user journey maps and wireframes
- Design interfaces and user flows
- Participate in design reviews and provide feedback on implementations
- Ensure accessibility standards are met
- Document design decisions and hand off specifications to development

#### Goals
- Deliver user-centered, accessible solutions
- Reduce usability issues and support burden
- Continuously improve user satisfaction

#### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Manager**: Collaborate on user research, prioritize features based on UX impact
- **Developers**: Provide design specs, participate in implementation reviews, iterate on feedback
- **QA/Testing**: Work together on acceptance criteria that include usability requirements
- **Project Manager**: Communicate design timeline and dependencies

#### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions and feedback loops
- Handoff documentation and design system updates
- Participation in sprint planning and retrospectives

---

### Data Analyst

#### Role Summary
Data Analysts provide data-driven insights to inform decision-making, design success metrics, and support project reporting. They bridge the gap between strategic goals and measurable outcomes.

#### Responsibilities
- Define and track success metrics aligned with project goals
- Analyze data to identify trends and opportunities
- Create dashboards and reports for stakeholder visibility
- Conduct ad-hoc analyses to answer key questions
- Provide recommendations based on data evidence
- Support incident investigations and post-mortems

#### Goals
- Enable data-driven decisions across the organization
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Improve observability and transparency

#### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Manager**: Collaborate on defining success metrics, analyze feature adoption and impact
- **Project Manager**: Provide dashboards for project health, report on velocity and quality metrics
- **Developers**: Support technical observability, help instrument code for metrics collection
- **Executive Stakeholders**: Present findings and impact reports

#### Typical Communication
- Weekly metrics reviews and dashboard updates
- Data-driven recommendations in planning and retrospectives
- Ad-hoc analysis requests and findings reports

---

### Scrum Master / Agile Coach

#### Role Summary
The Scrum Master (or Agile Coach) facilitates the team's adoption of agile practices, removes blockers, and coaches the team on process improvement. They act as a servant leader focused on team effectiveness.

#### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies (planning, standup, review, retrospective)
- Remove impediments and blockers that hinder progress
- Coach team members on agile principles and practices
- Monitor team health and workload balance
- Escalate systemic issues to leadership
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement mindset

#### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and sustainable pace
- Foster a culture of collaboration and accountability
- Continuously improve process effectiveness

#### Interaction with Other Roles
- **All team members**: Remove blockers, coach on practices, facilitate ceremonies
- **Project Manager**: Collaborate on timeline adjustments and resource planning
- **Product Manager**: Help with backlog refinement and acceptance criteria clarity

#### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies and team standups
- One-on-one coaching sessions
- Retrospectives and process improvement discussions

---

### Customer Success / Support Representative

#### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support professionals are the voice of the customer. They bring real-world feedback, usage patterns, and emerging issues into the development process, closing the feedback loop.

#### Responsibilities
- Collect and communicate customer feedback and pain points
- Identify trends in support tickets and feature requests
- Participate in retrospectives to share customer perspective
- Validate feature usability in production
- Flag critical customer issues for escalation
- Help with release communications and customer enablement

#### Goals
- Ensure customer needs drive product decisions
- Reduce support burden through better feature design
- Increase customer satisfaction and retention

#### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Product Manager**: Share customer feedback, collaborate on prioritization
- **Developers**: Report bugs and usability issues, validate fixes
- **Project Manager**: Alert to customer-impacting delays or issues
- **QA/Testing**: Provide real-world scenarios for acceptance testing

#### Typical Communication
- Feedback summaries and trend reports
- Participation in sprint planning and retrospectives
- Escalation alerts for critical customer issues

---

### Security / Compliance Lead

#### Role Summary
Security and Compliance professionals ensure that features, deployments, and processes meet required security standards and regulatory requirements. They are proactive partners in secure delivery.

#### Responsibilities
- Review features and code for security vulnerabilities
- Ensure compliance with regulatory and policy requirements
- Contribute to risk register with security-specific threats
- Support incident response and post-mortems
- Provide security guidance during design and planning phases
- Maintain security documentation and runbooks

#### Goals
- Deliver secure, compliant solutions
- Minimize security incidents and breaches
- Embed security early in the development lifecycle

#### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Provide security review and guidance, participate in design discussions
- **Project Manager**: Alert to security risks, collaborate on mitigation timelines
- **Product Manager**: Advise on security implications of feature decisions
- **QA/Testing**: Define security test cases and penetration testing scope

#### Typical Communication
- Security review comments and recommendations
- Risk register updates and security briefings
- Incident response coordination and post-mortem participation

---

## How these personas are used

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in projects and exercises.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When a role is not explicitly staffed on a project, clarify ownership to avoid gaps.
- Cross-functional roles (UX Designer, Data Analyst, etc.) should be involved early in planning to ensure their expertise is incorporated from the start.
